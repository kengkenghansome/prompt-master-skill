# Video Prompt Framework

## Core idea

Video prompts should be written like a director's brief.

A video is not just an image. It includes:

- subject
- scene
- action
- camera movement
- time
- rhythm
- atmosphere
- continuity

## Core formula

创意主题 + 视频时长 + 影片风格 + 主体设定 + 场景设定 + 情节动作 + 镜头语言 + 节奏控制 + 氛围情绪 + 细节强化 + 负面限制

## Required structure

### 1. 影片风格

Define the overall visual style.

Examples:

- cinematic realism
- commercial product film
- documentary style
- cyberpunk
- warm lifestyle film
- luxury brand visual
- animation style
- handheld documentary

### 2. 主体设定

Define the main subject.

Include:

- who / what it is
- appearance
- clothing
- state
- expression
- product features
- movement ability

### 3. 场景设定

Define where and when the video happens.

Include:

- location
- time of day
- weather
- background
- environmental elements
- spatial depth

### 4. 情节动作

Define what happens in the video.

Use a simple timeline:

- beginning
- middle
- ending

The action must be visible and sequential.

### 5. 镜头语言

Define how the camera captures the scene.

Include:

- shot size
- angle
- camera movement
- focus change
- transition
- close-up
- tracking shot
- push-in
- pull-out
- pan
- tilt
- handheld
- slow motion

### 6. 节奏控制

Define how the video rhythm changes.

Examples:

- slow opening, fast middle, calm ending
- steady commercial rhythm
- emotional gradual build-up
- high-energy short-form pacing

### 7. 氛围情绪

Define the emotional direction.

Examples:

- warm
- lonely
- futuristic
- premium
- peaceful
- dramatic
- mysterious
- energetic

### 8. 细节强化

Add visual details.

Include:

- light reflection
- fabric texture
- product material
- environmental motion
- wind
- rain
- dust
- bokeh
- facial expression
- sound feeling

### 9. 负面提示词

Add what should be avoided.

Examples:

- no distorted body
- no inconsistent face
- no flickering
- no unstable camera
- no random text
- no extra limbs
- no unnatural motion
- no abrupt scene jump

## Final video prompt template

请基于【创意主题】，生成一份适用于【XX】秒短视频的结构化视频生成 Prompt。

请按照以下结构输出：

影片风格：
【整体视觉风格、参考类型、影像质感】

主体设定：
【主要人物、物体、角色或产品的外观、状态、特征】

场景设定：
【时间、地点、环境、背景元素、空间氛围】

情节动作：
【视频中发生什么，动作如何开始、推进和结束】

镜头语言：
【景别、机位、镜头运动、推拉摇移、跟拍、特写、转场方式】

节奏控制：
【开头、中段、结尾的节奏变化】

氛围情绪：
【整体情绪、色彩倾向、音乐感、声音感】

细节强化：
【材质、光影、天气、人物表情、环境动态、产品细节】

负面提示词：
【不要出现的画面、风格、错误元素或不合理动作】

要求：
- 画面要有连续性
- 动作要明确
- 镜头要有变化
- 不要像静态图片拼接
- 适合直接复制到视频生成模型中使用
